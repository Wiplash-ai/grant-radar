Grant Grinder / Systems

Keep funding intelligence beside your work.

The toolbar button toggles a fixed-size Grant Grinder widget on the page you are using. Drag it by its header, close it when you need the space, or open the complete app in a new tab.

This controls where full searches and funding briefings open. Keep the official URL for normal use. Developers can use http://localhost:5173/search.

Privacy boundary

The extension injects only its isolated widget after you click the toolbar button. It sends submitted searches to Grant Grinder's public endpoint and, on Grant Grinder pages, can receive your favorites and saved searches from the existing first-party session. It never receives the session cookie, reads unrelated page content, or stores an API key.
